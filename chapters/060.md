## 60 — The Blind Flight of Love

> *The soul reaches God most deeply not by seeing, but by loving.*

One of the greatest challenges in speaking about contemplative prayer is that the deepest experiences cannot be fully described.

We can explain ideas. We can discuss theology. We can analyze methods of prayer. But there comes a point where words begin to fail. The realities of the spirit are often too subtle, too intimate, and too simple to be captured by language.

This is why the contemplative tradition speaks so often of a "blind" movement toward God.

The word does not mean ignorance. It does not mean confusion. It means that the soul is no longer relying primarily upon images, emotions, concepts, or detailed reflections. Instead, it reaches toward God through a simple movement of love.

The commentator observes that many people imagine prayer as a kind of affectionate gazing upon God. They picture the soul delighting in divine beauty much as a parent delights in a beloved child. The heart admires, rejoices, and remains occupied with the object of its affection.

There is certainly a place for such devotion.

Many beautiful forms of prayer arise from reflection upon God's goodness, Christ's compassion, or the beauty of divine love. These practices nourish the soul and help awaken affection for God. They remain precious gifts within the spiritual life.

Yet the contemplative path eventually moves beyond even these.

The soul becomes less occupied with looking at God and more occupied with yielding to God.

Less concerned with experiencing God and more concerned with belonging to God.

Less interested in spiritual feelings and more interested in simple fidelity.

The ancient author of *The Cloud of Unknowing* describes this as a blind stirring of love. The soul gathers the simple awareness of God that faith provides and then gently lifts itself toward Him. There are no elaborate images. No detailed reflections. No dramatic emotions required.

Only love.

A love so simple that the mind often overlooks it.

A love so quiet that it may seem like nothing is happening at all.

Yet beneath that simplicity, something profound is taking place.

The will is being drawn toward God.

The heart is consenting to God's presence.

The soul is learning to rest beyond the constant activity of thought.

This movement is often remarkably gentle.

The commentator speaks of the will quietly lifting itself toward God and remaining there as long as human weakness allows. There is no strain. No forcing. No attempt to manufacture spiritual experiences. The soul simply returns again and again to its loving intention.

And this return becomes its prayer.

Many people imagine that spiritual growth depends upon extraordinary experiences. Yet the contemplative tradition repeatedly teaches otherwise. The deepest transformation often occurs in hidden faithfulness. It unfolds in countless small acts of surrender, repeated day after day without fanfare.

The soul gradually becomes shaped by what it loves.

As love grows simpler, it also grows purer.

The heart becomes less attached to consolations, insights, and feelings. It begins to seek God not for what God gives, but for God Himself. The prayer becomes less about receiving and more about offering. Less about attaining and more about consenting.

And in that simplicity, a remarkable freedom emerges.

The soul no longer feels responsible for creating spiritual success. It learns to trust the hidden work of grace. It allows itself to be carried by a love deeper than understanding and steadier than emotion.

The commentator reminds us that many mystical writers describe this same reality using different words. Their language may vary, but their destination remains the same. They are all pointing toward a prayer that unfolds beyond analysis—a prayer where love becomes simpler, quieter, and more surrendered.

For the deepest realities of God cannot be grasped by thought alone.

They are received through love.

And when words finally reach their limit, love continues onward.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** *Lift my heart to You*
>
> **Exhale:** *Beyond thought, in love*
