## 60 - The Blind Flight of Love

> *The soul reaches God most deeply not by seeing, but by loving.*

The deepest movements of prayer are often the hardest to describe. Ideas can be explained, teachings can be studied, and methods can be practiced, but there comes a point where words begin to fall silent. The realities of the spirit are too intimate and too simple to be fully captured by language. What remains is not a lack of understanding, but a different way of knowing.

Contemplative prayer invites you into a gentle movement beyond images, emotions, and endless reflection. This does not mean abandoning devotion or the beauty of sacred thoughts. Rather, it means allowing love to become simpler. Instead of looking for God, you begin offering yourself to God. Instead of seeking an experience, you rest in a relationship.

This movement has often been called the blind flight of love. Faith gives you a simple awareness of God's presence, and love responds by reaching toward that Presence without needing to grasp or explain it. There are no special feelings required and no extraordinary experiences to attain. Only a quiet willingness to return your heart to God again and again.

Over time, this simple return begins to shape the soul. Love grows less dependent on comfort, insight, or emotional consolation. The heart gradually learns to seek God not for what can be received, but simply because God is worthy of love. What appears small and ordinary becomes a hidden work of transformation.

In this simplicity, a deep freedom emerges. You no longer carry the burden of creating spiritual success or manufacturing spiritual experiences. Instead, you learn to trust the steady work of grace and allow yourself to be carried by a love deeper than thought. When words finally reach their limit, love continues onward.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Lift my heart to You*
>
> Exhale: *Beyond thought, in love*
