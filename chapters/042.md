## 42 - Losing Yourself in Love

> *You find your deepest self not by clinging to it, but by offering it to Love.*

Some realities of the spiritual life can never be fully captured by words. They can be pointed toward, but not possessed by explanation. The deepest work of God within you unfolds in a place more intimate than thought, where understanding gives way to direct experience. As love grows, you begin to discover that what matters most is not knowing about God, but resting in God.

This holy union is not the loss of your personhood but its fulfillment. As your heart becomes more aligned with divine love, compassion deepens, humility grows, and peace takes root within you. These qualities are not achievements you add to your spiritual life. They are the natural fruit of a soul being quietly gathered into communion with God.

Contemplative prayer is part of this gentle gathering. As thoughts loosen their constant grip, the heart learns to turn toward God with greater simplicity. Fewer words are needed. The need to analyze every experience begins to soften. Like a flower opening toward the sun, the soul gradually learns to remain receptive to the Presence that is already shining upon it.

Along the way, you are invited to release not only your attachment to the world but also your attachment to the self you work so hard to defend. This is not self-rejection or harshness. It is the letting go of fear, false identities, and endless self-concern so that love may flow more freely. Grace accomplishes this work far better than effort alone ever could. Your task is not to manufacture holiness, but to remain open to the One who is already drawing you deeper.

As love matures, even your awareness of yourself begins to soften. You become less occupied with measuring your spiritual progress and more absorbed in the beauty of God. The heart learns to forget itself in adoration, not because the self is destroyed, but because love becomes its center. In this hidden surrender, you discover a freedom that cannot be achieved through striving—a quiet union where Love itself becomes your home.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Draw me beyond myself*
>
> Exhale: *Into the quiet of Your love*
