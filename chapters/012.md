## 12 - The Heart That Refuses Lesser Comforts

> *The soul begins to change when nothing less than God can satisfy its hunger.*

There comes a season when the heart begins to outgrow the things that once seemed enough. This does not make the world less beautiful. Relationships, work, laughter, and ordinary joys remain gifts to be treasured. Yet beneath them all, a deeper longing awakens—a hunger that no created thing can fully satisfy.

At first, you may try to quiet this longing through achievement, distraction, knowledge, or even religious activity. But eventually the soul discovers that the ache remains. What feels like restlessness is often love calling you deeper. Beneath every desire is a longing for the One who gives life to all things.

Rather than running from this ache, contemplation invites you to listen to it. The longing itself becomes a prayer, a quiet homesickness drawing you toward deeper communion. Like Mary Magdalene standing before the empty tomb, the heart learns that no substitute can fully satisfy what it seeks. Only the living Presence itself will do.

As this longing deepens, you become less concerned with explaining yourself to others. Some may not understand your desire for simplicity, silence, or inward prayer. Yet love no longer needs constant defense. You learn to trust that what is unfolding within you is held by God, even when it cannot be easily described or understood.

Alongside this longing, trust begins to grow. You slowly release the belief that everything depends upon your own striving and control. Grace arrives before you have all the answers. Strength appears when it is needed. And as your grip loosens, humility quietly blooms—not as self-rejection, but as the peaceful recognition that you are held within a love far greater than yourself.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Nothing less than Love*
>
> Exhale: *Can satisfy my soul*
