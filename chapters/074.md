## 74 - The One Thing Necessary

> *Love grows deepest when it forgets itself and rests entirely in God.*

As love matures within you, life begins to simplify. The heart becomes less occupied with its own fears, successes, and concerns and more attentive to the presence of God. This is not a rejection of yourself, but a release from constant self-preoccupation. Love gently shifts the center of gravity from the self toward the One who holds all things together.

This transformation cannot be forced. It unfolds naturally as the heart becomes captivated by something greater than itself. Like Mary sitting quietly in attentive presence, you learn that not every moment requires striving or accomplishing. Sometimes the most important thing is simply to be present before God with an open and willing heart.

From the outside, this way of prayer may appear unproductive, yet its fruits reach into every part of life. Love nourishes compassion, patience, humility, forgiveness, and generosity. The more deeply you rest in God, the more these qualities begin to flow naturally into your relationships and daily responsibilities. What is cultivated in secret gradually becomes visible in the way you live.

Over time, the soul learns to seek God not only for comfort, guidance, or blessing, but simply because God is worthy of love. Love becomes less concerned with what it receives and more concerned with communion itself. The heart discovers the joy of delighting in God for God's own sake. This is the purity toward which contemplation quietly leads.

As this trust deepens, you become less anxious about outcomes and more willing to rest in divine care. The uncertainties of life do not disappear, but they no longer carry the same weight. You learn the freedom of letting God be God. And in that freedom, love finds its deepest home.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *You alone are enough*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to love*
