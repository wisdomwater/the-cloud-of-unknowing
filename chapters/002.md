## 2 — The Cloud Between

> *Love reaches farther than understanding.*
>
> *God is often found where certainty disappears.*

Lift up your heart gently toward God.

Not with force.
Not with strained effort.
Not by trying to become impressive or spiritually accomplished.

Simply with love.

For I believe this longing within you did not begin with you alone. Long before you turned your attention toward the Divine, Love had already been quietly drawing you inward. Even through your wandering, your weariness, your distractions, something holy remained near—patiently calling you toward deeper rest.

And so do not think yourself special because you feel drawn to this path.

Rather, let this calling make you softer.
More humble.
More tender toward God and toward others.

For the soul grows not through spiritual ambition, but through surrender. If the Almighty bends low in love toward us, then let us also become lowly in heart. Let us loosen our grip on pride, comparison, and the need to appear certain.

This way is not about becoming greater than others.

It is about becoming open.

And now I must tell you something difficult, though it is also beautiful.

When you first begin this work of contemplation, you may not find immediate sweetness or clarity. You may sit quietly before God and feel only darkness. Your thoughts may wander. Your emotions may feel restless or numb. The mind may search desperately for understanding, yet find very little it can grasp.

Do not be troubled by this.

For this darkness is the cloud of unknowing.

It stands between you and God—not because God is absent, but because the Infinite cannot be contained by thought alone. The mind wishes to comprehend. It wishes to define and possess. But God is deeper than every image, every concept, every explanation the intellect can produce.

And so you must learn another way.

A quieter way.

You must rest beneath this cloud with a simple movement of love toward God. Not many words. Not endless analysis. Only a naked longing of the heart.

Remain there as best you can.

Even if you feel nothing.

Even if prayer seems empty.

Even if God feels hidden from you.

Stay.

For love is working even when the mind cannot perceive it.

This path asks very little outwardly, yet inwardly it asks for everything. It asks that you stop clutching after certainty. That you release the endless hunger to control your spiritual life. That you allow yourself to become small enough to enter mystery without fear.

And this may feel uncomfortable at first.

The modern mind wishes to measure progress. It wants immediate reassurance. It seeks experiences it can explain and hold onto. But contemplation slowly teaches the soul another rhythm altogether.

A slower rhythm.

A humbler rhythm.

The rhythm of remaining.

For God asks no great performance from you here. Only that you gently turn toward Him again and again, protecting the quiet doorway of your heart from the noise that scatters your attention.

Do this with patience.

Do this with gentleness.

And over time, you may discover something surprising:

the cloud itself becomes holy.

The darkness becomes a kind of shelter.

And what once felt like absence slowly becomes the hidden nearness of Love itself.
