## 2 — The Cloud Between

> *Love reaches farther than understanding.*
>
> *God is often found where certainty disappears.*

Lift your heart gently toward God.

Not with force.
Not through striving.
Not by trying to become spiritually impressive.

Only with love.

And perhaps even this longing within you did not begin with you alone.

Long before you turned toward God, Love may already have been drawing you inward quietly and patiently beneath your distractions, your weariness, your searching.

So do not cling to this path as though it makes you special.

Let it make you softer instead.

More humble.
More tender.
More willing to rest in what cannot be controlled.

For the soul does not grow through spiritual ambition.

It grows through surrender.

And this surrender can feel unfamiliar at first.

When you sit quietly before God, you may not feel peace right away. You may find restlessness instead. Thoughts moving constantly. Emotions rising and fading. A strange darkness where you expected clarity.

Do not be afraid of this.

The contemplatives called it the cloud of unknowing.

Not because God is absent.

But because God is deeper than thought.

The mind wants to grasp and define. It wants certainty it can hold onto. Yet the Divine cannot be contained inside explanations alone.

And so another way slowly opens.

A quieter way.

You begin learning how to remain with God without needing to fully understand.

Not through many words.
Not through constant analysis.

Only through a simple movement of love.

A gentle turning of the heart.

And this may feel very small.

You may wonder whether anything is happening at all.

But stay.

Even when prayer feels dry.
Even when your attention wanders.
Even when God feels distant.

Stay gently.

Love is still at work beneath what you can feel or perceive.

Over time, contemplation teaches the soul a different rhythm from the one the world often demands.

A slower rhythm.

One that no longer needs to measure spiritual progress so anxiously.

One that slowly releases the need to control every inner experience.

One that learns how to rest.

And little by little, the darkness itself begins to soften.

What once felt empty becomes quiet.

What once felt uncertain becomes spacious.

The cloud remains.

But now it begins to feel less like separation and more like shelter.

A hidden nearness.

A love too deep for words to hold.

## Breath Prayer

> Breath in: *Beyond my knowing*
>
> Breath out: *I rest in Love*
