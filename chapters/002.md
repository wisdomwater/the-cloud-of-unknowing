## 2 - The Cloud Between

> *Love reaches farther than understanding.*

Lift your heart gently toward God. Come without striving, without pressure, and without the need to become anything other than who you are. The soul does not awaken through effort alone, but through love. Even the longing that brings you here may already be a gift of grace quietly working within you.

As you follow this path, let it make you softer rather than more certain. The deeper you move into the mystery of God, the less need you feel to prove yourself or defend your spirituality. Love grows best in humble soil, and surrender nourishes the roots of the soul.

When you sit quietly before God, you may not find immediate peace. Thoughts may wander, emotions may rise, and a kind of inner darkness may seem to surround you. Do not be afraid. This cloud is not the absence of God, but the place where the mind reaches its limits and love learns to continue.

The mind longs to grasp and understand, yet God is deeper than any idea or explanation. Here you are invited to remain present without needing certainty. With each gentle return of attention, you offer a simple act of love to the One who is beyond all concepts and yet closer than your own breath.

Stay when prayer feels dry. Stay when nothing seems to happen. Over time, what once felt like emptiness becomes spaciousness, and what once felt uncertain becomes a quiet refuge. The cloud remains, but it no longer feels like separation. It becomes a hidden nearness, where love carries you farther than understanding ever could.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond my knowing*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Love*
