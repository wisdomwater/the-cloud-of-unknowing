## 17 - The Prayer Beneath All Words

> *Sometimes the deepest prayer is not many words. It is a single cry of the heart.*

As the contemplative life deepens, prayer often becomes simpler. The soul that once approached God with many thoughts, questions, and words begins to discover something quieter beneath them all. This is not a loss of devotion but a deepening of it. Love becomes less concerned with speaking about God and more concerned with resting in God's presence.

There are moments when even beautiful thoughts feel like too much movement. The heart no longer wishes to analyze every mystery or explain every feeling. Instead, it leans gently toward God with a longing that words cannot fully express. In this simplicity, prayer becomes more direct, more honest, and more deeply rooted in love.

Sometimes a single word is enough. A quiet whisper of "Love," "Mercy," "Peace," or "Jesus" can gather the attention of the heart and turn it toward God. The power is not in the word itself, but in the sincerity it carries. A small and simple prayer offered with love often reaches deeper than many distracted thoughts.

This can be a great comfort when prayer feels difficult. You do not need perfect focus, polished language, or strong emotions. A weary sigh, a silent turning, or a simple inward cry is enough. God is not waiting for performance. Love listens beneath the scattered thoughts, unfinished sentences, and restless emotions.

So when words feel heavy, let prayer become simple again. Offer whatever longing you have, however small it may seem. Turn your heart gently toward God and trust that you are already being heard. The quietest prayer may be the one that carries you deepest into Love.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Hear my quiet longing*
>
> Exhale: *Hold me close in Love*
