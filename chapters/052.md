## 52 - Knowing Through Unknowing

> *When every lesser knowing falls away, love remains awake.*

The journey into God is not a movement toward greater certainty but toward deeper trust. Like Moses ascending the mountain, you may pass through seasons of insight, understanding, and spiritual illumination. These gifts are good, yet they are not the final destination. Beyond every revelation lies an invitation into mystery itself.

There comes a point where familiar ways of knowing begin to grow quiet. The mind can no longer rely on explanations, images, or spiritual experiences to guide the way. What once seemed clear may feel hidden, and what once felt solid may become uncertain. Yet this darkness is not emptiness—it is the sacred cloud where God waits beyond the reach of ordinary understanding.

In this place, you begin to know differently. The intellect reaches its limit, but love continues onward. You discover that communion is deeper than analysis and presence is richer than explanation. Rather than holding God within your understanding, you allow yourself to be held within the mystery of God.

The contemplative life is often a work of subtraction rather than addition. Little by little, you release assumptions, expectations, fears, and attachments that obscure your vision. Like a sculptor revealing the image hidden within stone, grace gently removes what is unnecessary. As the coverings fall away, what has always been present begins to shine through.

What remains is the Presence beneath every thought, the Love beneath every longing, and the Mystery beneath every name. You discover that God was never absent, only hidden behind the many things you believed were necessary to find Him. And in the silence beyond understanding, a deeper knowing awakens—one that belongs not to the mind alone, but to the whole of your being.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond my understanding*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Your mystery*
