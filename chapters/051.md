## 51 - Beyond Every Word and Image

> *God is not found at the end of our thoughts, but beyond them.*

The journey toward God often begins with words. Scripture, prayer, sacred teachings, and holy images awaken the heart and help us turn toward the Divine. They are precious gifts, pointing us toward a reality greater than ourselves. Yet every word, no matter how beautiful, can only point beyond itself.

As love deepens, you begin to discover that God is more than any description you can hold. You may call God good, wise, loving, or merciful, and each of these is true. Yet the Divine always exceeds your understanding of goodness, wisdom, love, and mercy. What can be spoken is real, but it is never the whole.

This is why the soul must eventually learn the language of silence. Silence does not reject theology, prayer, or sacred reflection. Rather, it receives their gift and then moves beyond them. Words can bring you to the edge of mystery, but only surrender allows you to enter it.

The Scriptures often speak of God dwelling within cloud and darkness. This darkness is not absence but holy mystery—the hidden radiance of a Presence too vast for the mind to contain. Like Moses ascending the mountain, you may pass through signs, insights, and moments of revelation, only to discover that beyond them lies a deeper invitation. Beyond what can be explained, the heart encounters God directly.

Contemplative prayer invites you into this hidden place. The mind longs to grasp, define, and conclude, but love asks for something different. It asks you to trust what cannot be possessed and rest within what cannot be fully understood. There, beyond every word and image, the soul discovers that it has always been held within the mystery of Love.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond every word*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Your mystery*
