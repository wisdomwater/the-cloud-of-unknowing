## 3 — Love Knows What Thought Cannot

> *The mind reaches outward.*
>
> *Love enters inward.*

There are different ways the soul reaches toward God.

Part of you wants to understand. It wants clarity, meaning, certainty. This is not wrong. The mind is a gift. Through it you learn, reflect, discern, and encounter the beauty woven throughout creation.

But there comes a point along the contemplative path where understanding can no longer lead the way.

For God is greater than thought.

The mind tries to reach upward, searching for language large enough to contain the Infinite. Yet every explanation eventually becomes too small. Every image fades at the edges. The deeper mystery of God always remains beyond what the intellect can fully hold.

And this is not failure.

It is invitation.

For while thought eventually reaches its limit, love continues onward.

Love knows how to remain near what it cannot fully explain.

This is one of the quiet discoveries contemplation slowly teaches the heart.

You do not need perfect understanding in order to draw near to God.

You do not need brilliant insight.
You do not need emotional certainty.
You do not need to force spiritual experiences into being.

Sometimes all that is needed is a small inward turning.

A gentle movement of love toward the Divine.

And often this movement feels far quieter than people expect.

You may sit in prayer and feel distracted. Your thoughts may wander endlessly. Silence may seem empty at first. You may wonder whether anything meaningful is happening beneath the surface at all.

But do not rush away too quickly.

The hidden work often begins there.

Not in mastery.
Not in dramatic experiences.

But in simple willingness.

A willingness to remain present before God without demanding immediate clarity.

A willingness to return again after distraction.

A willingness to sit beneath the cloud without trying to force it open.

And over time, these small returnings begin changing something within you.

The scattered parts of the soul slowly gather together.

The constant striving softens.

Prayer becomes less about achieving something and more about consenting to Presence.

Even brief moments begin to matter.

A quiet breath offered toward God.
A small pause in the middle of a hurried day.
A moment of surrender instead of control.

These may appear insignificant from the outside.

But love measures differently.

And if sorrow rises within you as you notice how distracted or restless your life has been, hold that sorrow gently.

Do not turn it into despair.

The contemplative path is not built upon shame for the past. It is built upon returning.

Again and again, returning.

For Christ does not meet you only as judge or observer.

Christ meets you as healer.

As companion.

As the quiet Presence already waiting beneath all your wandering.

And so even now, you are not beginning alone.

Others have walked this hidden path before you. Quiet souls. Unknown saints. Tender hearts who also learned slowly how to rest in divine love beyond certainty and explanation.

You belong to this great unfolding communion more than you realize.

And each small turning toward Love becomes part of your transformation.

Not all at once.

But gently.

Like dawn slowly filling a darkened room.

## Breath Prayer

> Breath in: *Love draws me inward*
>
> Breath out: *Beyond thought, into God*
