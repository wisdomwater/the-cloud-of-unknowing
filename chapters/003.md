## 3 - Love Knows What Thought Cannot

> *The mind reaches outward. Love enters inward.*

The mind is a beautiful gift. Through it you learn, reflect, discern, and seek understanding. Yet there comes a point on the contemplative path where thought can no longer lead the way, for God is always greater than any idea, image, or explanation the mind can create.

This is not a failure of understanding but an invitation into a deeper way of knowing. When thought reaches its limits, love continues onward. Love does not require certainty before it draws near. It simply rests in the presence of the One it cannot fully comprehend.

You do not need perfect insight or extraordinary spiritual experiences to come close to God. A simple turning of the heart is enough. Even when your thoughts wander, your attention drifts, or prayer feels empty, something sacred is happening each time you gently return.

Over time, these small acts of returning begin to change you. The scattered parts of your soul slowly gather together, and the need to strive softens. Prayer becomes less about achieving something and more about consenting to a presence that has always been waiting for you.

If you become aware of your restlessness or the ways you have wandered, meet that awareness with kindness. The contemplative path is not built upon shame but upon returning again and again to love. Like the slow light of dawn filling a darkened room, each small turning toward God becomes part of your transformation.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Love draws me inward*
>
> Exhale: *Beyond thought, into God*
