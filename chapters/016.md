## 16 - Let Love Work Within You

> *You do not create the deepest prayer. You consent to it. You allow yourself to be carried.*

There comes a point when you realize that the deepest movements of prayer cannot be produced through effort alone. The mind longs for methods, certainty, and control, but contemplation gently loosens that grip. You begin to discover that what is most sacred often unfolds beyond the reach of force. Love becomes less something you achieve and more something you receive.

As this awareness grows, humility naturally follows. You stop trying to explain every movement of grace or measure your spiritual progress. Even your longing for God begins to look different. You realize that the desire to seek, pray, and return is itself a gift already stirring within you. Something deeper has been calling you all along.

Because of this, the contemplative life becomes less about striving and more about consent. Rather than forcing transformation, you learn to cooperate with it. You become willing to rest in the quiet work already taking place beneath the surface. Like a tree slowly shaped by the seasons, your growth unfolds through patience, trust, and time.

This changes how you relate to your own imperfections. When distractions arise, you simply return. When weakness appears, you return again. Prayer is no longer another burden placed upon an already weary soul. It becomes a place of rest where you learn to trust that God is working within you even when you cannot clearly see the results.

You do not need to understand the whole mystery before taking the next step. It is enough to keep turning toward the quiet attraction of Love. Little by little, your inner world is reshaped—not through pressure or performance, but through presence. The deepest prayer is not something you accomplish. It is something you allow.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Teach me how to yield*
>
> Exhale: *To the work of Love*
