## 19 - Letting Go of Yourself

> *The deepest surrender is not the loss of your humanity. It is the loosening of your grasp upon it.*

As the contemplative journey deepens, prayer begins drawing you beyond constant concern with yourself. In the beginning, much of spiritual life revolves around your healing, growth, struggles, and understanding. This is natural. Yet over time, the heart discovers a freedom that comes from no longer making yourself the center of every thought, fear, or desire.

You begin to notice how much suffering comes from clinging too tightly to your own stories and need for control. The anxious self works endlessly to protect its image, secure certainty, and defend its place in the world. Contemplation gently reveals how exhausting this can be. The soul starts longing for something larger than self-preservation.

This surrender is not self-rejection. You are not called to despise your humanity, your emotions, or your individuality. Rather, you are invited to release the false sense of separateness that keeps you trapped in fear and striving. Love does not ask you to become less human. It invites you to become more fully alive by resting in a deeper belonging.

At times, this awakening carries a holy sorrow. You become more aware of your fears, attachments, and inner fragmentation. Yet this sorrow is not meant to crush you. Held gently, it softens the heart and opens hidden places to grace. Healing comes not through harshness but through tenderness.

Beneath it all, a deeper longing begins to emerge—the desire to belong completely to Love. Little by little, the grip of the false self loosens. You stop striving so hard to hold everything together and discover that you are already being held. Quietly and patiently, Love continues its work within you, drawing you beyond yourself and into communion.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Loosen my fearful grasp*
>
> Exhale: *Draw me into Love*
