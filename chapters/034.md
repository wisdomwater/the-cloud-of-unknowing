## 34 - The Quiet Work of Grace

> *Grace does not hurry the soul. It unfolds gently, according to love rather than comparison.*

One of the subtle temptations along the spiritual path is the temptation to compare your journey with someone else's. You wonder whether your prayer is deep enough, whether your progress is fast enough, or whether others have discovered something you have somehow missed. Yet contemplation is not a competition, and the soul does not grow according to a timetable. Grace unfolds differently within each person because each heart carries its own story, wounds, gifts, and lessons in surrender.

For some, stillness arrives slowly after years of longing and inner healing. For others, silence feels familiar from the beginning. Neither path is better. Neither soul is more loved. What matters is not how quickly you awaken, but your willingness to remain open to the work Love is doing within you today.

The seasons of prayer also change. There are times when silence feels dry, distracted, or difficult, and times when peace seems to arise effortlessly from within. Both belong to the journey. Like tides along a shoreline or seasons beneath the sky, the inner life moves according to rhythms deeper than the mind can fully understand.

Because of this, patience becomes a sacred practice. You cannot force awakening through effort, comparison, or spiritual ambition. The more you try to grasp it, the more it slips away. Grace works gently, inviting trust rather than achievement, presence rather than performance.

As you learn to trust this unfolding, something begins to soften within. The need to become someone else fades. The urge to chase dramatic experiences loses its power. Instead, you simply remain available to God in this moment, allowing grace to shape your soul in its own time. Like a lamp burning quietly through the night, you learn to wait without striving, trusting that the light is already on its way.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Teach me patient trust*
>
> Exhale: *Grace unfolds in its time*
