## 69 - The Humility of Not Being the Center

> *True humility is not thinking less of yourself, but resting in something greater than yourself.*

You did not create yourself, sustain yourself, or bring yourself into being. Every breath, every moment, and every gift of life flows from a Source beyond your own strength. While this may begin as an idea, contemplative prayer slowly transforms it into a lived reality. The heart discovers that it does not need to carry the burden of being its own foundation.

This is the beginning of true humility. Humility is not self-rejection, shame, or the denial of your worth. It is the joyful recognition that your life is rooted in something infinitely greater than yourself. As love deepens, the constant need to place yourself at the center of every concern begins to loosen, and a quiet freedom emerges.

In moments of contemplation, the ordinary preoccupation with yourself may fade into the background. The heart becomes still, spacious, and attentive to God's presence. Yet these moments are not an escape from daily life. You return to ordinary responsibilities carrying a little more wisdom, tenderness, and trust than before.

Spiritual growth unfolds through many cycles of clarity and obscurity, nearness and distance, ascent and return. Each season offers another opportunity to surrender, trust, and love. Little by little, the grip of self-concern softens, and virtues such as patience, compassion, and gentleness begin to grow naturally within you.

As the self releases its need to be the center of everything, you become available to something far greater. Love reshapes the heart from within, and humility becomes less an achievement and more a way of being. When words become complicated and certainty fades, love remains a faithful guide, continually drawing you back to the One who has always been sustaining you.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *You are the source*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in You*
