## 44 - The Doorway and the Call

> *Walk the path given to you, and trust God with the rest.*

The contemplative life begins not with extraordinary experiences but with a simple invitation. Christ is the doorway through which you enter the mystery of God. By reflecting on His compassion, His humility, His suffering, and His love, your heart is gradually opened to a deeper trust. This path is not reserved for a spiritual few; it is available to anyone willing to approach with sincerity and humility.

There is no shame in remaining near this doorway. Many souls spend their lives drawing nourishment from the life and teachings of Christ, growing in faith, gratitude, and love. God does not measure your worth by how far inward you travel or how profound your spiritual experiences become. What matters is your willingness to respond faithfully to the grace you have been given today.

The temptation is to become impatient. The ego longs for spiritual achievement, special experiences, and a sense of progress it can measure. Yet contemplation cannot be conquered through effort or spiritual ambition. The deepest movements of prayer are gifts, received rather than attained. The humble path may seem ordinary, but it provides the steady foundation upon which deeper love can grow.

As your journey unfolds, you may discover a quiet longing awakening within you. Not a restless desire to become special, but a gentle hunger for a deeper awareness of God's presence. If that invitation comes, receive it gratefully. If your path leads instead through service, family, teaching, work, or acts of mercy, receive that with equal gratitude. Love expresses itself through many callings, and none are greater than another.

The contemplative heart eventually learns to stop comparing, measuring, and demanding explanations. God knows how to guide each soul according to its unique needs. Your task is not to manage the journey but to walk it faithfully. As trust deepens, the heart relaxes its grip and discovers a quiet confidence that Love is already leading the way.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Lead me in Your wisdom*
>
> Exhale: *I trust the path You give*
