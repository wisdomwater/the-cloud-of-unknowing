## 47 - Beyond Knowing Into Love

> *The mind may point the way, but love alone can enter the mystery.*

There comes a season when thoughts about God can no longer carry you where God wishes to lead. The reflections, insights, and teachings that once nourished your faith remain valuable, but they begin to feel incomplete. What once served as a doorway now invites you to step through it. The heart senses that God is calling it beyond understanding into a more direct experience of presence.

This transition can feel unsettling. The mind naturally wants something to grasp—a concept to examine, a question to answer, or an experience to analyze. When those familiar supports become less satisfying, prayer may feel empty or uncertain. Yet this apparent emptiness is often an invitation, drawing you from thinking about God toward simply resting with God.

The contemplative path does not reject knowledge; it places knowledge in its proper role. Wisdom illuminates the path, but love walks it. You can study a fire, describe a fire, and understand a fire, yet none of those things is the same as standing in its warmth. In the same way, the soul is transformed not merely by understanding divine love, but by surrendering to it.

At times this surrender feels less like peace and more like struggle. You sit in silence and encounter distractions, resistance, and wandering thoughts. One part of you longs to remain present while another seeks escape. Yet even within that struggle, something important has changed. Your heart has chosen its direction. Though imperfectly, it continues turning toward God.

Over time, a quiet simplicity begins to emerge. Prayer becomes less about achieving an experience and more about offering your presence. The soul discovers a hidden sweetness in this faithful surrender, finding rest not because every struggle disappears, but because love has become its center. In this way, grace gently teaches what words never could, leading you beyond knowing and into communion.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Teach me beyond knowing*
>
> Exhale: *Lead me into love*
