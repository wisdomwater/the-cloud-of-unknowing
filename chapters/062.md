## 62 - The Quiet Turning of the Heart

> *Love carries you where striving cannot go.*

Every movement toward God begins in grace. Long before you become aware of your longing, Love is already drawing you closer. The spiritual life is not a journey you create through effort alone, but a response to a Presence that has been calling you from the beginning.

There are seasons when study, reflection, and deliberate prayer help awaken and steady the heart. These practices are good gifts, but they are not the destination. Over time, you may discover that thinking about God no longer satisfies the deepest hunger within you. The heart begins longing not for more understanding, but for deeper communion.

As this invitation unfolds, you may find yourself drawn toward a simpler way of being with God. Old fears loosen their grip, endless analysis begins to quiet, and love becomes more natural than striving. This is not something you achieve. It is something you receive as you learn to trust the gentle work of grace within you.

You cannot force this deepening any more than a flower can force itself to bloom. Your task is not to control the journey but to remain open, patient, and willing. God knows the shape of your soul and leads you according to a wisdom far greater than your own.

As love matures, the heart learns to rest in mystery. It no longer needs to understand everything in order to move forward. Love reaches where understanding cannot, and it continues to rise even when the path disappears from view. In that quiet turning toward God, you slowly become what you love.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Draw my heart toward You*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to trust*
