## 73 - Love Knows What the Mind Cannot

> *The heart can rest in God long before the mind understands Him.*

The spiritual journey begins with learning about God through prayer, reflection, sacred stories, and contemplation. These gifts awaken desire and teach the heart how to seek what is holy. Yet there comes a time when thoughts and images can carry you no further. You begin to discover that God is not merely an idea to understand, but a living presence to love.

This deeper invitation should never be forced. The mind remains a valuable companion, and every stage of prayer has its purpose. But as love matures, your attention gradually shifts from thinking about God to simply resting in Him. The heart becomes quieter, not because it has found all the answers, but because it has learned to trust the mystery.

The mind can grasp many truths about God, yet God always remains greater than every thought, image, or explanation. Love reaches where understanding cannot because it is willing to remain present without possessing certainty. This is the wisdom of contemplation: not solving the mystery, but dwelling within it. The heart learns that reverent unknowing can be a form of deeper knowing.

Your life is shaped by the direction of your love. Each time you choose to turn toward God, even after wandering, something within you is being transformed. Progress may seem hidden, but sincere intention matters. The repeated desire to seek, return, and love gradually reshapes the soul from within.

As love deepens, humility begins to grow naturally. You become less occupied with yourself and more captivated by the beauty and goodness of God. In those moments, humility feels spacious rather than heavy, freeing rather than diminishing. For a little while, self-concern falls away, and you discover the simple joy of being held within a love greater than yourself.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond what I know*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to love*
