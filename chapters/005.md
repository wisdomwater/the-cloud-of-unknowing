## 5 - A Simple Word for God

> *You do not need to carry many thoughts into prayer. Sometimes one small word is enough.*

As you enter prayer, you may notice how quickly the mind fills with thoughts. Some are beautiful and meaningful, stirring devotion, gratitude, or longing for God. These reflections have their place, for they help awaken the heart and teach it how to love.

Yet there comes a time when contemplation invites you into a deeper quiet. Not away from love, but beyond the need to analyze every spiritual insight or feeling. The mind naturally seeks understanding, but love is content simply to remain present.

When thoughts arise during prayer—even holy ones—you do not need to resist them or follow them. Let them pass gently, and return your attention to God. Again and again, you practice this simple movement of returning, not with force but with tenderness.

A single sacred word can help anchor the heart in this quiet consent. Perhaps it is God, Love, Peace, or another word that draws you inward. Not as a formula to repeat mechanically, but as a gentle reminder of the presence you seek to rest within.

Over time, this faithful returning begins to reshape you. The mind loosens its need to control, the heart grows comfortable with silence, and prayer becomes less about having an experience and more about abiding in love. You sit, you return, and through this hidden simplicity, the soul slowly learns how to rest in God.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *God of quiet love*
>
> Exhale: *Gather me into You*
