## 21 - The Quiet Depth of Prayer

> *God is often found most deeply in the still places of the soul.*

As prayer deepens, it often becomes simpler. What begins as effort slowly softens into trust. You no longer feel the need to find the perfect words or manufacture the right emotions. Love already knows your heart before you speak.

Nothing must be proven to God. Nothing must be displayed to earn divine attention. As this realization settles within you, prayer becomes less about doing and more about being. The heart learns to rest quietly in a Presence that has always been near.

Beneath the noise of thoughts, feelings, and endless explanations, there is a deeper place where love simply abides. Prayer becomes a gentle turning toward God again and again. Not a striving to reach the Divine, but a willingness to remain available to Love.

The body and emotions remain sacred companions on the journey. Tears, tenderness, silence, and whispered prayers all have their place. Yet contemplation teaches you not to measure spiritual depth by intensity of feeling. Love is often doing its deepest work when nothing dramatic seems to be happening at all.

As you grow steadier in God, the need for spectacle begins to fade. You become less concerned with signs, experiences, and certainty, and more rooted in humility, patience, and trust. The deepest prayer is often wonderfully ordinary: a quiet heart resting in Love, allowing that Love to slowly transform you from within.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Quiet my striving*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to rest in Love*
