## 37 - The Simple Knowing That You Are

> *The soul comes nearest to God when it stops trying to become more than it already is before Him.*

There is a kind of weariness that comes from constantly analyzing yourself. The mind searches through wounds, failures, and hidden motives, hoping to discover peace through greater understanding. Yet beneath all the thoughts about who you are lies a simpler truth: you are. Before every success and failure, before every fear and hope, you already exist in the presence of God.

This humble awareness is enough to begin contemplation. You do not need special knowledge or spiritual accomplishments. You do not need to solve yourself before approaching God. Simply becoming aware of your own existence before the One who gives you life can open a deeper doorway than endless self-examination ever could.

There is a place for repentance and honest self-knowledge, but there comes a time when continually turning over your brokenness no longer brings healing. Instead of remaining absorbed in your imperfections, gently turn toward Love. Bring your unfinished, imperfect self into God's presence just as you are. Healing comes not because you understand yourself completely, but because you allow yourself to be touched by mercy.

The contemplative life is far simpler than the mind expects. Again and again, thoughts will search for techniques, explanations, and spiritual achievements. Yet love often grows deepest when it becomes simpler than thought. In quiet prayer, the soul stops scattering itself among countless concerns and rests before the One who holds all things together.

As you rest in this simplicity, something within begins to soften. You are no longer striving to force your way toward God or earn His nearness. Instead, you discover that you are already being held. The journey becomes less about becoming someone else and more about allowing yourself to be gathered into the Love that has been sustaining you all along.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *I rest before You*
>
> Exhale: *As I am*
