## 78 - Holding Lightly, Loving Deeply

> *The soul finds freedom when it uses the world without trying to possess it.*

You are called to live fully within the world without becoming bound by it. Relationships, work, rest, possessions, and responsibilities all have their place, yet none of them can carry the weight of your deepest longing. The problem is rarely the gifts themselves, but the tendency to cling to them as though they were your true home. Love invites you to receive them with gratitude while keeping your heart rooted in God.

When your center remains in God, life becomes lighter. You can enjoy what is given without needing to possess it, and release what is lost without losing yourself. The gifts of life become blessings rather than burdens. They are no longer asked to provide what only divine love can offer.

This path does not require constant self-criticism or endless analysis of every mistake. You will sometimes become distracted, attached, or forgetful, and that is part of being human. The invitation is not to obsess over every failure, but to notice it, receive grace, and return. Love grows through returning, not through self-condemnation.

As love deepens, you become less preoccupied with yourself. The endless concerns about image, comfort, success, and control begin to loosen their grip. For brief moments, you discover the freedom of simply resting in God's presence without striving to prove, protect, or improve yourself. In those moments, the soul remembers that it was never meant to carry the whole world on its shoulders.

The contemplative life is ultimately simple. Rather than chasing experiences or endlessly evaluating your spiritual progress, you learn to trust the quiet work of grace. A steady desire for God, a willingness to surrender, and a heart that keeps returning are enough. The soul does not become whole by becoming more complicated, but by becoming more available to love.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Help me hold lightly*
>
> Exhale: *And love deeply*
