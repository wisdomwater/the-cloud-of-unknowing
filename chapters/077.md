## 77 - The Freedom of a Heart Fixed on God

> *When the heart is centered in God, everything else finds its proper place.*

The spiritual life becomes simpler as love deepens. What once seemed complicated gradually gives way to a single invitation: return your heart to God. Again and again, beneath thoughts, emotions, and circumstances, grace draws you toward a deeper center. There, in the quiet depths of the soul, you discover a stillness where God has been waiting all along.

From this hidden place, even the smallest movement of love becomes meaningful. A brief prayer, a moment of surrender, or a silent turning of the heart can carry great power. God is not concerned with perfect words or methods. What matters is the sincerity of your desire and the willingness of your heart to remain open to divine love.

Because of this, there is great freedom in prayer. Some days love may express itself through words, and other days through silence. At times prayer may overflow into gratitude, tears, song, or simple stillness. The form is secondary. What matters is whether your prayer arises from love and draws you toward greater humility, peace, and trust.

As the soul matures, love becomes less an occasional act and more a steady orientation. You cannot remain consciously in prayer every moment, yet your life can remain directed toward God even while engaged in ordinary responsibilities. Beneath work, rest, relationships, and daily concerns, a deeper longing continues to flow. Prayer becomes not merely something you do, but the direction in which your heart is moving.

When love becomes the center, much of life's anxiety begins to loosen its grip. You no longer need to manage every detail through constant self-monitoring and worry. By seeking God first, wisdom gradually grows in its own time. Little by little, you discover the freedom of a heart that knows where its true home is and trusts everything else to find its proper place around that center.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Center my heart*
>
> Exhale: *In Your love alone*
