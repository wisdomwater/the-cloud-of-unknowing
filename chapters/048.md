## 48 - Restoring the Divine Image

> *You do not become divine by striving. You awaken to the image already planted within you.*

Deep within you is an imprint of the Divine that can never be erased. Life may cover it with fear, distraction, ambition, or countless attachments, but it remains present beneath the surface. What you give your attention to shapes your experience of yourself, yet no season of wandering can destroy what God has woven into your being. The image remains because it is part of who you are.

The spiritual journey is not about creating a new self but uncovering the deeper self that has always existed in God. Like an ancient icon hidden beneath years of dust, your true nature may become obscured, but it is never lost. Contemplative prayer gently clears away what conceals this inner beauty. Little by little, the light already present begins to shine more freely.

As grace works within you, the whole person begins to heal and awaken. Faith teaches the heart to trust what it cannot fully see. Hope helps you lean toward God even when the path feels uncertain. Love gathers every part of your life into a deeper communion with the One who created you. These are not merely virtues to acquire but signs that the divine image is becoming more visible through you.

This transformation is usually quieter than we expect. Rather than dramatic experiences or extraordinary revelations, it often appears as greater patience, deeper compassion, and a growing freedom from fear. The false identities built from striving and self-protection slowly loosen their grip. What emerges is not someone new, but someone more fully alive.

The contemplative life does not pull you away from your humanity; it restores it. As you become more aligned with divine love, you do not become less yourself but more truly yourself. Each act of trust, each movement of hope, and each surrender into love reveals a little more of the image that has always been there. The journey is simply the gradual awakening to what God has already planted within you.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Restore Your image within me*
>
> Exhale: *Shape me in Your love*
