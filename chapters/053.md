## 53 - Revealing What Has Always Been Hidden

> *The soul does not create the light. It removes what obscures it.*

Much of the spiritual journey begins with the feeling that something is missing. You may search for God through study, prayer, discipline, or deeper understanding, hoping to discover what seems distant or hidden. Yet the deeper truth is that God is not absent. What needs to change is not God's nearness, but your ability to recognize the Presence that has always been here.

The contemplative path is less about acquiring something new and more about releasing what stands in the way. Like a sculptor uncovering a figure hidden within stone, grace gently removes the layers that conceal what is already present. Fear, distraction, attachment, and endless striving slowly fall away. Beneath them, something beautiful begins to emerge.

This work cannot be forced. It unfolds patiently as you learn to loosen your grip on certainty, self-image, and the need to control your spiritual experience. The deeper self, rooted in God, does not need to be created or perfected. It simply waits to be revealed as the unnecessary layers are stripped away.

Along the way, even your most sacred ideas about God must be held lightly. Words such as goodness, wisdom, mercy, and love point toward what is real, but they can never contain the fullness of the Divine. After speaking comes silence. After understanding comes wonder. After seeking comes surrender.

What remains is not emptiness but Presence. Beneath every thought, every longing, and every breath, the light of God quietly shines. As the soul settles into stillness, it discovers that what it sought was never far away. The light was always here, waiting beneath everything you thought you were.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Remove what obscures*
>
> Exhale: *Reveal Your hidden light*
