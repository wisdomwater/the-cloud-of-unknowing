## 35 - Holy Desire Never Truly Leaves

> *The longing for God is itself a quiet sign that grace is already near.*

The contemplative life cannot be created through effort or sustained through spiritual ambition alone. It begins as a response to grace already moving within you. While not every soul is drawn to contemplation in the same way, some discover a quiet longing that continues returning throughout the years—a desire for stillness, silence, and deeper communion that refuses to disappear completely.

This longing is more important than it may appear. It is not necessarily dramatic or emotional. Often it feels like a gentle ache, a persistent turning of the heart toward something deeper. And the longing itself is a gift, a sign that Love is already at work within you long before you fully understand where it is leading.

Every contemplative journey passes through seasons when God's presence feels hidden. Prayer may seem dry, silence empty, and the heart weary. In these moments, it is easy to believe something has gone wrong. Yet love often deepens in hidden ways during seasons of waiting. What feels like absence may actually be preparing the soul for a deeper trust that is no longer dependent upon feelings alone.

This is the way of every enduring relationship. There are seasons of closeness and seasons of silence, yet love continues growing beneath the surface. Like roots reaching deeper into the soil, holy desire often matures through delay. What is shallow fades when fulfillment is postponed, but what is genuine becomes stronger and more resilient.

Over time, you begin to see that even your longing for God is already a form of communion. The desire to seek, to return, and to begin again is itself evidence of grace. So do not judge yourself harshly when certainty fades or prayer feels difficult. If the longing remains, however faintly, Love has not abandoned you. It is quietly drawing you home, and in your unknowing, you are already known.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Keep holy desire alive within me*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to trust Your timing*
