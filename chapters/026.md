## 26 - The Humility That Does Not Perform

> *True humility forgets itself in Love.*

The desire to grow spiritually is beautiful, yet it can quietly become tangled with the desire to be seen as spiritual. You may find yourself hoping to appear wise, peaceful, or deeply awakened in the eyes of others. The ego is remarkably subtle, and it can turn even sincere devotion into another way of seeking approval. Yet love invites something much simpler.

As the heart matures, it becomes less concerned with how it is being perceived. Humility is not trying to appear humble. It is the freedom that comes when you no longer need to manage your image so carefully. Prayer gradually loosens the need to impress and teaches you how to rest in what is true.

Many of us learned early in life to earn belonging through performance. We tried to be pleasing, capable, moral, or admirable in order to feel secure. These patterns can follow us into our spiritual lives, turning prayer into another attempt to prove our worth. But contemplation gently teaches that you are already held in love before you accomplish anything at all.

As this truth sinks deeper into the soul, something begins to relax. The need to curate yourself softens. Your words become more natural, your listening more attentive, and your presence less strained. Love becomes less about appearance and more about honest presence before God and others.

The fruits of this humility are quiet but beautiful. You no longer need to convince anyone that you are close to God. Instead, compassion grows, patience deepens, and peace becomes woven into ordinary life. True humility does not draw attention to itself. It simply rests in Love and allows that Love to shine through.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Free me from performance*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me honest Love*
