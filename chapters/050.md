## 50 - Entering the Hidden Darkness

> *To receive the Infinite, the soul must loosen its grip on everything it can hold.*

As you are drawn deeper into prayer, there comes a time when even the things that once helped you find God must be held more lightly. Sacred teachings, beautiful ideas, cherished experiences, and meaningful insights all have their place. They guide the heart and awaken love. Yet none of them can fully contain the One toward whom they point. Eventually, the soul is invited to rest in God rather than in its understanding of God.

This invitation leads into what the contemplatives call holy darkness. It is not a darkness of fear or emptiness, but a mystery that lies beyond the reach of ordinary knowing. Here, you release the need to grasp, define, and control. The mind gradually loosens its hold on explanations, and the heart learns to trust what it cannot fully comprehend.

At first, this can feel uncomfortable. We naturally seek security in what we can perceive and understand. We want clear answers and reliable certainty. Yet God is not an object to be examined from a distance. The deeper you journey into divine mystery, the more you discover that God exceeds every image, thought, and category the mind can create.

This does not mean abandoning wisdom or embracing ignorance. Rather, it means allowing humility to take the lead. Every doctrine, symbol, and spiritual insight serves as a doorway, not a destination. They point beyond themselves toward a living Presence that can never be reduced to words. Love becomes a deeper form of knowing, not because it understands more, but because it participates more fully.

When you become willing to enter this sacred unknowing, you discover that the darkness is filled with light. Beneath the silence is a Presence more intimate than thought and more real than imagination. There, beyond the grasping of the intellect, the soul is quietly drawn by love into deeper communion with God. What seemed like darkness reveals itself as a hidden radiance all along.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond all that I know*
>
> Exhale: *Draw me into Your mystery*
