## 67 - Faithfulness in the Dark

> *Even when you cannot feel love, love may still be quietly at work within you.*

There will be seasons when prayer feels dry and God's presence seems distant. The warmth you once knew may fade, and your heart may feel unable to reach for Him as it once did. In those moments, it is easy to believe that something has gone wrong. Yet silence is not abandonment, and dryness is not the absence of grace.

Often God's deepest work takes place beneath the surface of your awareness. Even when you feel incapable of loving, a hidden faithfulness remains. Each time you return to prayer, endure the silence, or quietly offer yourself to God, love is still at work. What appears empty may conceal a profound surrender of the heart.

During these seasons, love takes on a different form. It becomes less about feelings and more about trust. Instead of consolation, it expresses itself through patience, perseverance, and the willingness to remain present without reward. You begin to discover that love is not merely an emotion but a steady fidelity to the One you seek.

These difficult seasons also reveal what has been mixed into your devotion. As comforts disappear, you learn to love God not for the peace He gives, but for His own sake. Though the path may feel lonely, it often becomes a doorway into greater freedom. The heart slowly releases its need for certainty and learns to rest in trust alone.

Over time, prayer becomes simpler. You no longer need to measure your progress or analyze every inner movement. Instead, you learn to remain available to God, trusting that grace is working even when you cannot perceive it. Every act of faithfulness in the dark prepares your soul for a deeper freedom, where love remains steady whether it is felt or not.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *When I cannot feel*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to trust*
