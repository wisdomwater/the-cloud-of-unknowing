## 66 - Beyond Images Into Mystery

> *Love carries the soul where thought can no longer follow.*

You first come to God through words, images, ideas, and understanding. These are good gifts that awaken faith and help guide your heart. Yet as love deepens, you begin to discover that God is greater than every image you can imagine and deeper than every thought you can hold.

This is the meaning of the cloud of unknowing. It is not a barrier separating you from God, but the humble recognition that God cannot be contained by the mind. As you gently release your attachment to concepts, explanations, and even cherished spiritual images, you make room for a deeper encounter with the living Mystery they were always pointing toward.

Alongside this is the cloud of forgetting. For a little while, you lay aside distractions, worries, ambitions, and everything that pulls your attention away from Love. You are not rejecting the world but releasing your grip upon it. In that surrender, the heart becomes more available to God's presence.

This deepening cannot be forced. Grace leads the way, quietly drawing you beyond striving and into trust. Your part is simply to respond, allowing God to shape your heart over time through faithfulness, surrender, and love. Spiritual growth is less about mastering a technique and more about becoming receptive to a Presence already at work within you.

As love matures, your motives gradually become simpler and purer. You begin seeking God less for comfort, answers, or spiritual experiences and more because your heart has fallen in love with Him. Again and again, you return to Love for its own sake. And what once seemed like darkness slowly reveals itself to be a holy mystery where the soul feels most at home.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond every image*
