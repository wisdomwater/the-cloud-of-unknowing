## 11 - The Love That Cannot Be Measured

> *Some forms of prayer disappear with time. Love alone remains.*

There is a quiet freedom that grows within you when you no longer need everyone to understand your inner life. Not everyone will recognize the beauty of stillness or the longing that draws you toward silence and prayer. Many can only speak from what they themselves have experienced. When you remember your own blindness in earlier seasons, compassion begins replacing the need to be understood.

As the heart matures, it becomes less concerned with defending itself. You learn that not every sacred thing needs explanation. Some truths are better lived than argued. The quietness itself becomes part of the prayer, teaching you to trust what God is doing within you even when others cannot see it.

Love expresses itself in many ways. Sometimes it serves meals, carries burdens, and tends faithfully to the needs of daily life. Sometimes it sits quietly, listening and receiving. Both belong to the spiritual life, yet beneath every act of service and every moment of prayer lies the same invitation: to turn the heart toward God in love.

As you continue along this path, many things that once seemed important begin to loosen their hold. There are seasons for learning, striving, questioning, and understanding, but eventually thought reaches its limits. Words grow thin at the edge of mystery, and the soul stands before God with empty hands. There, beyond explanation, love remains.

Again and again, you simply return. You sit before God as you are, releasing the need to grasp, perform, or fully understand. Slowly the heart becomes more spacious, more peaceful, and less divided within itself. Beneath the noise of the world, you discover a love that cannot be taken from you because it is already rooted in eternity.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Teach me to rest in Love*
>
> Exhale: *Beyond all understanding*
