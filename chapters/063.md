## 63 - The Longing That Leads Home

> *Love finds its way to God through longing rather than understanding.*

The deepest movements of the soul begin with desire. Beneath every prayer, every search, and every longing is the quiet pull of Love drawing you home. The contemplative life is not primarily about mastering a method, but about allowing your heart to turn again and again toward God.

In the beginning, reflection, study, and meditation help awaken this turning. They train your attention and deepen your awareness of God's presence. Yet there often comes a season when thinking about God gives way to something simpler. The mind becomes quieter, and love begins to lead where words and ideas can no longer go.

This deepening rarely happens in a straight line. There are times when prayer feels effortless and wordless, and other times when familiar practices become necessary again. Be patient with these rhythms. God is not measuring your progress but gently teaching your heart to trust.

Over time, the soul begins to turn toward God more naturally, like a flower opening toward the sun. Prayer becomes less about achieving an experience and more about consenting to a Presence already near. The longing itself becomes a gift, for every desire for God carries within it a promise of deeper communion.

There will be moments when longing gives way to quiet rest and the heart simply abides in Love. When those moments pass, do not grieve them. Each return of longing becomes another invitation to seek, trust, and surrender. Little by little, you discover that the One you have been seeking has been drawing you home all along.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *My heart longs for You*
>
> Exhale: *You are my home*
