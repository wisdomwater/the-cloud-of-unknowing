## 64 - The Holy Longing

> *The soul finds peace not by possessing God, but by continually turning toward Love.*

The longing you feel for God is already a gift of grace. Whenever your heart reaches beyond comfort, certainty, or achievement, something deeper is calling you home. This holy desire is not a sign of absence but of Presence, drawing you toward a communion not yet fully seen but already known within.

There are moments when this longing settles into a quiet rest. The heart becomes still, and for a time you simply abide in the love you have been seeking. Yet when that stillness passes and the longing returns, nothing has been lost. The rhythm of seeking and resting is part of the soul's growth in love.

Do not be discouraged by the return of desire. Each longing enlarges the heart and deepens its capacity to receive. What feels like repetition is often transformation unfolding beneath the surface, preparing you for a fuller surrender to God.

As love matures, prayer becomes simpler. You no longer need to chase every thought, solve every question, or cling to every image that arises. Instead, you gently place distractions aside and return your attention to God. Again and again, you choose love over whatever would pull your heart away.

This path unfolds slowly and with great tenderness. There is no need to compare your journey with anyone else's or rush toward some imagined destination. Your task is simply to follow the light given to you today, trusting that every sincere movement of love is leading you deeper into God. The holy longing itself is evidence that Love is already near.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Awaken holy desire*
>
> Exhale: *Draw me into Love*
