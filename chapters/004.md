## 4 - The Cloud Beneath You

> *What you release gently begins to loosen its hold.*

There are moments in prayer when your heart turns naturally toward God. For a brief time, the inner noise grows quiet, and you find yourself resting in a simple awareness of divine presence. Then, almost as quickly, thoughts return—memories, worries, plans, and unfinished conversations pulling at your attention once again.

Do not be discouraged by this. The contemplative path is not about maintaining perfect focus or eliminating every distraction. It is about gently returning, again and again, to the love beneath all the movement of the mind. Each return, no matter how small, becomes part of the prayer itself.

As you continue, you begin to discover that God cannot be grasped through thought alone. The mind longs for certainty, explanations, and clear understanding, but love is willing to rest inside mystery. This is the cloud of unknowing, where you release the need to fully comprehend and simply remain present.

At the same time, another cloud forms beneath you—a cloud of forgetting. For a little while, you loosen your grip on your worries, plans, judgments, and even your ideas about spiritual progress. You are not rejecting these things; you are simply setting them down long enough to be present with God.

Over time, this gentle practice softens something deep within you. The need to control begins to relax, the heart grows less afraid of silence, and the soul learns a quieter trust. When distractions come, return gently. Beneath all the noise, Love remains present, patiently receiving you again and again.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *I release what I carry*
>
> Exhale: *I return to Love*
