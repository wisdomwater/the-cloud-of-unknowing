## 61 - The Way Most Souls Walk

> *God often works most deeply through ordinary faithfulness.*

You do not need extraordinary experiences to walk closely with God. Much of His work unfolds quietly beneath the surface of your awareness, like roots growing unseen beneath the soil. Love often deepens in hidden ways long before you can recognize its fruit.

At times, you may sense yourself being drawn toward God without knowing how to explain it. The heart is lifted by a gentle movement of grace that words cannot fully capture. Some things are learned not through understanding but through surrender and participation.

Your path will not look exactly like anyone else's. Some grow through silence, some through prayer, some through seasons of joy, and others through hardship. Yet beneath every path, the same Love is at work, patiently drawing each soul toward deeper union.

Even the burdens you would never choose can become part of this holy formation. Sorrow, disappointment, limitations, and unanswered questions can soften the heart and teach compassion, humility, and trust. Nothing is wasted when it is placed into the hands of God.

Do not become distracted by the pursuit of unusual spiritual experiences. The sacred invitation is simply to return each day—to pray, to love, to trust, and to begin again whenever you wander. A life steadily surrendered to Love often grows deeper than a life spent chasing spiritual excitement.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Lead me in Your way*
>
> Exhale: *I trust Your guidance*
