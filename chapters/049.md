## 49 — The Light Hidden in Holy Darkness

> *The deepest mysteries are not grasped by the mind, but received in silence.*

Eternal Wisdom, Source of all that is, beyond every name and every image, draw us toward Yourself.

You are the beginning without beginning and the end without ending. You are the goodness behind every good thing, the beauty behind every beautiful thing, and the life hidden within all life. Though You are nearer to us than our own breath, You remain beyond the reach of every thought and every definition.

And so we come not as masters seeking to understand, but as pilgrims willing to be led.

Lead us beyond the limits of our own understanding. Lead us beyond the restless need to explain, analyze, and possess. Lift us gently toward that sacred mystery where words grow quiet and the soul learns to listen. For there are depths within You that cannot be entered through effort alone. They open only to love, humility, and surrender.

The ancient contemplatives spoke of a holy darkness.

Not the darkness of fear, confusion, or absence, but a darkness created by overwhelming light. Just as eyes cannot gaze directly into the brilliance of the sun, so the mind cannot fully comprehend the radiance of God. What appears to us as darkness is often the soul standing before a mystery too vast for thought to contain.

And yet, within this darkness, God shines.

There is a silence deeper than words. There is a knowing beyond concepts. There is a presence that cannot be grasped, yet can be deeply experienced. In these moments, the soul ceases trying to capture God with ideas and instead rests in simple awareness, allowing itself to be held by the One it cannot fully understand.

This sacred unknowing is not ignorance.

It is reverence.

It is the humility that recognizes that God will always be greater than our finest thoughts, larger than our highest theology, and more beautiful than anything imagination can create. The closer we come to God, the more we discover that mystery is not a problem to solve but a reality to enter.

And in that mystery, something wonderful happens.

The soul is gradually filled with a light that cannot be seen with physical eyes. A clarity emerges that is not dependent on explanations. Peace begins to grow where certainty once demanded answers. Love becomes content to rest in what it cannot fully comprehend.

This is the invitation of contemplative prayer.

To enter the silence.

To release the need to grasp.

To trust the One who dwells beyond every image and every word.

And there, in the hidden darkness of divine love, to discover a radiance that has been waiting for us all along.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** *Draw me into mystery*
>
> **Exhale:** *I rest in Your light*
