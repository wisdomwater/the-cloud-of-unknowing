## 49 - The Light Hidden in Holy Darkness

> *The deepest mysteries are not grasped by the mind, but received in silence.*

God is nearer to you than your next breath, yet forever beyond the reach of your thoughts. No word can fully contain Him, no image can perfectly reveal Him, and no explanation can exhaust His mystery. The spiritual journey begins when you stop trying to master the Divine and become willing to be led. Like a pilgrim entering unknown territory, you learn to trust what cannot yet be understood.

As contemplation deepens, you may find yourself standing before what feels like darkness. This is not the darkness of absence or abandonment, but the darkness that comes from overwhelming light. Just as your eyes cannot gaze directly into the brilliance of the sun, your mind cannot fully comprehend the fullness of God. What appears to be unknowing is often the soul encountering a reality greater than thought can hold.

In this holy darkness, a different kind of knowing begins to emerge. You stop trying to capture God with ideas and instead learn to rest in His presence. The heart becomes quiet. The need for constant answers softens. Rather than grasping for certainty, you allow yourself to be held by the mystery itself.

This sacred unknowing is not ignorance but reverence. It is the humble recognition that God will always be greater than your understanding. The closer you draw to Him, the more you discover that mystery is not a problem to solve but a reality to enter. Love becomes willing to trust where knowledge can no longer lead.

Over time, this surrender gives rise to a deeper clarity. Peace grows where certainty once demanded control. A quiet light begins to shine within, not because every question has been answered, but because your heart has learned to rest in the One who holds them all. In the hidden darkness of divine love, you discover a radiance that has been present from the very beginning.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Draw me into mystery*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Your light*
