## 79 - Softly Toward God

> *Love grows best when it is gentle.*

Many people begin the spiritual journey with great intensity. They long for healing, transformation, and a deeper experience of God, and they assume that greater effort will bring greater results. Yet the contemplative path invites a different kind of strength. Rather than striving to seize God through force, you are invited to turn toward divine love with gentleness, trust, and openness.

This gentleness is not weakness. It is the quiet strength of a heart that no longer needs to control everything. Like a flower turning toward the sun or a river flowing toward the sea, the soul learns to move naturally toward God. The desire remains sincere and wholehearted, but it is no longer frantic or strained.

As prayer deepens, you begin to discover the limits of effort. There are some things that cannot be forced into existence. Love cannot be manufactured. Trust cannot be commanded. Presence cannot be achieved through willpower alone. The heart learns to consent to grace rather than trying to control it.

Along the way, you may experience moments of emotional warmth, joy, or spiritual excitement. These can be beautiful gifts, but they are not the foundation of the journey. Feelings rise and fall, while love remains. The deeper work of prayer is found in a steady willingness to remain turned toward God through every season, whether consolation is present or absent.

This is the quiet strength of contemplation. It is built not upon extraordinary experiences, but upon faithful availability. Joy and dryness, certainty and mystery, comfort and uncertainty may all come and go. Yet love continues its gentle turning toward God. And over time, that patient, unforced love transforms the soul more deeply than any moment of intensity ever could.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Teach me to trust*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to soften*
