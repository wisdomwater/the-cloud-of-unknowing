## 22 - Love Beyond Feeling

> *The deepest love remains even when sweetness fades.*

There are seasons when prayer feels warm and alive. Peace settles over you, tears come easily, and the nearness of God seems unmistakable. These moments are gifts, and they can nourish the heart in beautiful ways. Yet they are not the foundation upon which love is built.

As you continue along the contemplative path, you begin to discover that love can remain even when those feelings grow quiet. Prayer is no longer sustained by emotional comfort alone, but by a simple willingness to keep turning toward God. Beneath changing moods and shifting experiences, a deeper faithfulness begins to take root.

The heart slowly learns not to depend upon constant reassurance. Some days feel bright and full of consolation, while others feel ordinary or hidden. Yet love does not disappear when sweetness fades. The quiet choice to remain present, to offer your inward yes once again, is itself a sacred act of prayer.

Receive moments of spiritual comfort with gratitude when they come, but do not cling to them. Love becomes freer when it is no longer grasping for a particular feeling or experience. You do not need extraordinary visions, sensations, or certainty to be close to God. The desire to belong to Love is already a form of communion.

Trust that much of the deepest transformation happens beyond what you can feel or measure. Even when prayer seems dry, something holy may be unfolding beneath the surface. Love continues its patient work within you, inviting you into a steadiness that no changing emotion can take away.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Teach me steady love*
>
> Exhale: *Beyond every feeling*
