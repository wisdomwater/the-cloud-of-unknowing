## 6 - Love Beyond Understanding

> *There is a kind of knowing that only becomes possible when you stop trying to grasp.*

The mind is a gift. Through it you learn, reflect, discern, and grow in wisdom. Yet there is a difference between thinking about God and resting in God. Contemplation begins when you loosen your need to understand everything and become willing simply to remain present.

The mind naturally seeks clarity and certainty. Even in prayer, it fills the silence with ideas, reflections, and questions. Many of these thoughts are beautiful and nourishing, yet they are still smaller than the One you seek. Love can continue where understanding reaches its limits.

At first, this can feel unsettling. Silence may seem empty, and the mind may wonder whether you are doing enough. But the spaciousness of contemplation is not absence. It is an invitation to trust that God is present even when you cannot explain, measure, or fully comprehend what is happening.

As you continue returning to stillness, something begins to soften within you. The pressure to achieve certainty loosens, and the need to control every spiritual experience gradually fades. Prayer becomes less about seeking insight and more about consenting to a presence that is already here.

Over time, you discover that God was never waiting for perfect understanding. The invitation has always been simpler than that. Remain available. Stay open. Return when distracted. In this quiet faithfulness, the heart learns to trust love more than control and to rest within a mystery that is already holding you.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond thought, You remain*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Your presence*
