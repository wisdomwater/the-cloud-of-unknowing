## 39 - The Holy Simplicity of Being

> *God is found most deeply when the soul rests beyond its need to explain Him.*

The spiritual life often begins with names, images, and teachings that awaken love within the heart. We speak of God as loving, merciful, wise, and holy, and each of these points toward something true. Yet no word can fully contain the mystery of the Divine. There comes a moment in contemplation when the soul stops reaching for better explanations and simply rests before the reality of God's presence.

This same simplicity is offered to you as well. You do not need to sort through every strength, weakness, success, or failure before approaching God. Beneath all the complexity of your story lies a quieter truth: you exist. And your existence itself is already held within the sustaining presence of divine Love.

Every breath is a gift continually received. You were not self-created, nor do you sustain yourself by your own power. Your life rests moment by moment within the One who gives life to all things. Contemplation becomes the simple act of entrusting your whole being back to the Source from which it came.

As the mind loosens its grip on endless analysis, a deeper wisdom begins to emerge. Not the wisdom of arguments or certainty, but the wisdom that comes through silence, surrender, and love. Some truths cannot be mastered by thought alone. They are received gently, like sunlight spreading across still water.

From this hidden communion, love naturally flows outward. The heart becomes softer, steadier, and more gracious toward others. What happens inwardly begins shaping the outward life without strain or performance. The soul finds peace not by possessing God through understanding, but by allowing itself to rest within the Love that has always held it.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *You are*
>
> Exhale: *And I rest in You*
