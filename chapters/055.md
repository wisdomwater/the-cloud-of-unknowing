## 55 - The God Beyond Every Name

> *Every name for God is true, yet God is greater than every name.*

As you grow in contemplation, you begin to discover that God cannot be contained by any image, idea, or definition. Creation reveals glimpses of the Divine everywhere—in beauty, wisdom, love, strength, and wonder. These are precious reflections of God’s presence, but they are not God Himself. They are windows that open toward a reality far greater than anything they reveal.

The mind naturally creates images of God that feel familiar and understandable. You may imagine God through the lens of your experiences, hopes, fears, and expectations. Yet the Divine is always more than the pictures you create. The contemplative path gently loosens your attachment to these inner constructions so that you can encounter the living reality beyond them.

As this surrender deepens, you begin releasing even your most beautiful spiritual concepts. God is not merely wise as you understand wisdom, nor loving as you understand love. Every quality you admire finds its source in God, yet God's reality infinitely surpasses your understanding of it. Each sacred name points toward the Divine while reminding you that the mystery remains greater still.

This is why contemplation becomes less about mastering ideas and more about consenting to Presence. The need to explain gives way to wonder. The desire for certainty softens into trust. Instead of trying to hold God within your understanding, you learn to rest within a mystery that lovingly holds you.

There, beyond every image, doctrine, and name, the soul discovers a Presence that is both intimate and immeasurable. God cannot be possessed or fully explained, yet can be deeply known through love. The more you release your grasp on what you think God is, the more open you become to who God truly is.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond every name*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Your mystery*
