## 71 - The Hidden Work of Love

> *The deepest movements of the soul begin as a quiet longing for God.*

The contemplative life begins with a desire that seems small at first—a gentle turning of the heart toward God. You do not create this longing through effort or spiritual achievement. It is a gift already stirring within you, drawing you toward the One from whom your life has come. As you respond to it with faithfulness, this hidden desire gradually becomes the center around which everything else begins to gather.

This longing is deeper than emotion. Feelings rise and fall like waves, bringing seasons of warmth, joy, dryness, and uncertainty. Yet beneath these changing experiences, the heart can remain firmly turned toward God. Some of the purest love is found not in moments of spiritual excitement, but in quiet faithfulness when nothing seems to be happening at all.

The world often measures value by visible results, but the work of love unfolds largely in secret. When you offer your heart to God, even in silence, something holy is taking place. Love enlarges the soul. As your love for God deepens, so does your capacity to love other people with greater compassion, patience, and tenderness.

You do not leave the world behind through contemplation. Rather, you learn to love the world through God. In resting within divine love, you begin to carry others with you, even when no words are spoken. Love itself becomes prayer, and the heart quietly participates in God's care for all creation.

As you continue along this path, there comes a time when trust must carry you beyond what the mind can grasp. Images, explanations, and certainties gradually give way to a deeper faith. You learn that God is closer than every thought about Him, yet always beyond your ability to contain Him. The mystery remains, the cloud remains, but so does love—and love is enough.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Awaken holy longing*
>
> Exhale: *Draw me into love*
