## 18 - The Simple Prayer of the Heart

> *The soul does not always need many words. Sometimes longing itself is prayer.*

As the soul matures, prayer often becomes simpler. In the beginning, words help you express your hopes, fears, questions, and desires before God. These prayers are good and necessary. Yet over time, contemplation draws you toward something quieter, where love speaks more directly than explanation ever could.

A sincere longing for God often carries more depth than many carefully chosen words. When the heart becomes honest, it no longer feels the need to perform or impress. Sometimes all that remains is a simple inward cry: "Help," "Mercy," "Peace," or "Stay." These small prayers become doorways through which the whole heart turns toward Love.

Much of the contemplative path involves gathering your scattered attention and returning it to a single center. The mind naturally divides, analyzes, compares, and worries. But beneath all that activity lies a deeper place of rest. There you discover that God is greater than thought, and that love can reach where understanding cannot.

As you learn this simplicity, you become gentler with yourself. You stop measuring every flaw, emotion, or spiritual struggle so relentlessly. Instead, you bring your whole self into the presence of God and trust that healing can happen without constant self-analysis. The virtues you seek begin to grow naturally from this deeper communion, like fruit growing from healthy roots.

So keep returning, whether prayer feels clear or clouded, peaceful or restless. You do not need to force holiness into existence or carry your spiritual life through sheer effort. Love works more quietly than that. Each sincere turning of the heart, however small, is already being welcomed and held in mercy.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Gather my scattered heart*
>
> Exhale: *Into simple Love*
