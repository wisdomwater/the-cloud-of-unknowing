## 9 - Love Draws You Higher Than Shame

> *The soul is not healed by endlessly staring at its darkness. It is healed by turning toward Love.*

It is possible to become so focused on your failures that they begin to define your spiritual life. You replay old mistakes, measure your shortcomings, and search constantly for what is wrong within you. While honest self-awareness has its place, contemplation reveals that true humility is not found in endless self-condemnation.

There are times when sorrow and repentance soften the heart and open it to grace. Yet you are not meant to remain forever bowed beneath the weight of your past. At some point, love asks you to lift your gaze again. The goal is not to ignore your wounds, but to allow them to be held within something greater than shame.

As love deepens, you begin to discover a humility born from awe rather than fear. You realize that your life is sustained by grace, not by your own perfection. The heart becomes less preoccupied with proving its worth and more attentive to the presence of God that remains near through every weakness and failure.

Healing rarely comes through endless analysis alone. It comes through returning—returning to God, returning to love, returning to the quiet presence beneath your fears and defenses. Each time you turn again, you strengthen your trust in grace rather than in self-judgment.

Over time, longing becomes stronger than shame. You bring your small love, your imperfect attention, and your willingness to remain. Little by little, the soul grows less fearful and more open to communion. Love becomes the force that draws you forward, and you discover that it is love, not shame, that keeps you near to God.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Lift my heart toward Love*
>
> Exhale: *Beyond shame, into grace*
