## 28 - God Is Not Somewhere Far Away

> *Love discovers the Presence already near.*

Many people imagine God as distant, hidden somewhere beyond reach. Prayer then becomes a kind of striving, an attempt to bridge the gap between where you are and where God must be. Yet contemplation gently reveals a different truth. The Presence you seek is not far away, but nearer than you realize.

As the soul matures, it begins to release the need for spiritual effort and imagination. You no longer have to manufacture extraordinary experiences or climb toward God through thought. Prayer becomes simpler. It becomes a quiet openness to the One who is already here, already sustaining every breath and every moment.

This does not mean symbols, stories, and sacred images lose their value. They can awaken wonder, tenderness, and longing within the heart. But they are not the destination. They are windows pointing beyond themselves toward a Reality greater than any image, idea, or explanation can contain.

The deeper contemplative path teaches you to hold these sacred things lightly. Rather than clinging to concepts, you learn to rest within mystery. Not an empty mystery, but a living one—a Love that can be known and experienced even when it cannot be fully understood. The mind grows quieter, and the heart becomes more receptive.

And often, it is in this simplicity that something beautiful is discovered. God was never absent. Only hidden beneath the noise of striving, fear, and endless searching. As that noise begins to settle, the Presence that has always surrounded and filled your life quietly comes into view.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *You are nearer than I imagine*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me to rest in Presence*
