## 80 - Beyond Every Image

> *God cannot be contained by any picture the mind can create.*

As your journey with God deepens, you may begin to discover that every image, concept, and description eventually reaches its limit. The mind naturally creates pictures to help understand spiritual realities, and these can be beautiful and helpful guides. Yet God is always greater than the images that point toward Him. What you seek is not far away in some distant place, but nearer than your own breath and deeper than your deepest thought.

The imagination is a gift, but it cannot carry you all the way into the mystery of God. There comes a point when words become too small, concepts become too narrow, and explanations become incomplete. Rather than grasping for greater certainty, the heart is invited to rest in a deeper trust. Love continues where understanding can go no further.

This is the wisdom of the cloud of unknowing. When familiar images begin to fall away, you are not entering emptiness but mystery. You are discovering that God cannot be possessed by the mind because God is not an object to be examined. The divine presence is encountered not through control or comprehension, but through surrender, openness, and love.

Along this path, you may also discover a new freedom. As attachment to labels, roles, achievements, and spiritual experiences loosens, the soul becomes more spacious and at rest. You learn to appreciate theology, reason, and imagination without mistaking them for God Himself. They remain valuable guides, but they are not the destination.

Your journey will unfold in its own unique way, shaped by the wisdom and grace that know exactly what your soul needs. There is no need to compare your path with another's or measure your progress against anyone else. The invitation remains wonderfully simple: remain faithful, let go of the need to grasp, and continue returning to love. Beyond every image and beyond every certainty, you will discover the Presence you have been seeking all along.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond all images*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Mystery*
