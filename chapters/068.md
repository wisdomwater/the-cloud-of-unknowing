## 68 - Losing Yourself in Love

> *The soul becomes most itself when it no longer clings to itself.*

One of the great mysteries of the spiritual life is that you find freedom not by becoming more absorbed in yourself, but by becoming less preoccupied with yourself. In the beginning, much of your attention naturally revolves around your thoughts, emotions, struggles, and progress. Over time, however, love gently shifts your focus. Your heart becomes less concerned with itself and more captivated by God.

This transformation unfolds slowly through countless acts of surrender, trust, and faithfulness. Little by little, the need to constantly evaluate, defend, or define yourself begins to soften. Prayer becomes simpler, and you discover moments when you are less entangled in thought and more settled in the quiet awareness of God's presence.

This does not mean losing your identity or rejecting your humanity. Rather, you are being freed from the endless habit of making yourself the center of every experience. The self remains, but it no longer carries the weight of being the center around which everything revolves. Your attention becomes gathered, your heart becomes quieter, and love becomes more natural.

As this deepening continues, you begin to sense life from a larger horizon. Personal concerns still matter, but they no longer dominate the landscape of your soul. You discover that your truest life is rooted in something greater than yourself, held within a Love that has always surrounded and sustained you.

What first feels like losing yourself becomes the discovery of your deepest self. The contemplative journey is not the erasure of who you are but the liberation of who you are. And in that freedom, you learn to love more deeply, live more fully, and rest more completely in God.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Free me from myself*
>
> Exhale: *Root me in Your love*
