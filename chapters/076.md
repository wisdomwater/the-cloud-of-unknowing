## 76 - Returning Again to God

> *The wandering mind is not defeated by force, but by returning to love.*

The spiritual life is not a journey toward perfect concentration. Thoughts will arise, memories will surface, and worries will seek your attention. This is part of being human, not a sign that something has gone wrong. The invitation of contemplation is not to eliminate distractions, but to gently return your heart to God whenever you notice you have wandered.

When distractions appear, there is no need for frustration or self-condemnation. Fighting with thoughts often gives them more power than they deserve. Instead, allow them to be present without becoming captivated by them. Like clouds passing through the sky, they can remain for a time while your deeper attention continues turning toward the One who waits beyond them.

Even thoughts about your failures can become a distraction when they keep you trapped in endless self-examination. There is a time for honesty, repentance, and healing, but there is also a time to let go and move forward. God does not call you to remain forever focused on your brokenness. Love invites you through your wounds and into deeper communion.

The longing that keeps drawing you back is itself a gift of grace. It does not depend upon emotional intensity or spiritual excitement. It remains present through dry seasons, difficult days, and moments when prayer feels small or imperfect. Beneath every distraction lives a quiet willingness to return, and that willingness is often enough.

You are not making this journey alone. The desire to seek God arises because God is already seeking you. Each time you turn back with a simple prayer, a whispered word, or a silent act of love, you are responding to a presence that has never left. So when distractions come, do not be discouraged. Look beyond them, and return again to love.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Beyond every distraction*
>
> Exhale: *I return to You*
