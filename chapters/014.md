## 14 — Returning Beneath the Noise

> *The mind may wander endlessly.*
>
> *Love simply returns.*
>
> *Again and again.*

There are moments on the contemplative path when something opens unexpectedly.

After long seasons of silence, surrender, confusion, or dryness, a sudden stillness may appear within you. Not something dramatic. Not always emotional. Sometimes it arrives quietly, like a soft light moving through fog.

For a brief moment, the noise falls away.

You become aware of a Presence deeper than thought.

A tenderness beyond explanation.

A kind of inward knowing that cannot fully be spoken aloud.

The ancient contemplatives often described these moments carefully because they understood how fragile language becomes near mystery. The experience itself is not something the mind can control or reproduce on command. It comes like grace always comes:

freely.

Gently.

Unexpectedly.

And because of this, contemplation slowly teaches humility.

You begin realizing that the deepest movements of prayer are not achievements you manufacture through effort or spiritual talent. They are gifts. Openings. Brief unveilings of the Love that has been quietly surrounding you all along.

Your part is not to force these moments into existence.

Your part is simply to remain available.

To return.

To wait with sincerity.

To place your attention gently toward God again and again, even when nothing seems to happen.

And this returning matters more than you know.

Because much of contemplative prayer feels very ordinary from the outside. You sit in silence. Thoughts arise. Distractions pull at you. Old memories surface. Restlessness presses in. At times, it may even seem like nothing is changing at all.

But beneath the surface, something patient is unfolding.

The roots of the soul are slowly growing deeper.

And often, this deepening happens through repetition more than intensity.

You return after distraction.

You return after discouragement.

You return after failure.

You return after old wounds rise up once more.

Not harshly.

Not with self-condemnation.

But with quiet faithfulness.

The contemplative path is not about becoming someone who never struggles with thoughts, emotions, or weakness. It is about learning not to live entirely captive to them.

Thoughts will continue to appear.

Memories will continue to rise.

The mind will continue searching for certainty, control, distraction, and escape.

This is part of being human.

But little by little, you begin discovering that you do not have to follow every thought that enters the mind. You can let many things pass beneath the cloud of forgetting for a little while. You can stop feeding every fear with endless attention.

And this creates space.

Space for stillness.

Space for love.

Space for God.

At first, this work can feel difficult and tiring. The mind resists silence because it has spent years learning constant movement. Many people become discouraged when they first encounter how noisy their inner world truly is.

But this discovery is not failure.

It is awakening.

You are finally beginning to see what has always been happening beneath the surface.

And slowly, through patient practice, silence becomes less frightening. The heart learns it can survive without constant stimulation. The nervous system begins softening. You become less reactive to every passing emotion or mental storm.

Not perfectly.

But gradually.

And along the way, another beautiful thing begins happening:

compassion.

For once you begin seeing your own restlessness clearly, it becomes harder to judge others harshly. You recognize how much hidden struggle exists inside every human being. The contemplative path softens the instinct to condemn because you become more aware of your own need for mercy.

So you stop spending so much energy measuring who is holy and who is not.

Who deserves grace and who does not.

You leave more room for mystery.

For healing.

For the possibility that God may be working invisibly within people you do not fully understand.

And all the while, beneath the noise, beneath the thoughts, beneath the endless movements of the mind, Love continues waiting for you patiently.

Not demanding perfection.

Only willingness.

## Breath Prayer

> Breath in: *I return again to You*
>
> Breath out: *Beneath the noise within me*
