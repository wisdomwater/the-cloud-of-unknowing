## 14 - Returning Beneath the Noise

> *The mind may wander endlessly. Love simply returns again and again.*

There are moments when the noise within you briefly grows quiet and a deeper stillness emerges. You become aware of a Presence that cannot be captured by thought or fully explained with words. These moments are not earned through effort or spiritual achievement. They arrive as gifts, gentle reminders that Love has been near all along.

Because of this, contemplation teaches humility. The deepest movements of prayer cannot be controlled or manufactured. Your task is not to force an experience but to remain available. You simply return, offering your attention to God whether you feel close or far, clear or distracted.

Much of this returning feels ordinary. Thoughts arise, memories surface, and the mind pulls in a hundred directions. Yet every time you notice and gently return, something is being strengthened within you. The roots of the soul grow deeper through faithfulness more than intensity.

Over time, you begin to see that you do not have to follow every thought that enters your mind. You can let fears, worries, and distractions pass without giving them complete control. Space begins to open within you—space for stillness, space for mercy, space for God. What once felt like failure slowly reveals itself as part of the practice itself.

As you become more aware of your own restlessness, compassion naturally grows. It becomes harder to judge others harshly when you see how much patience you yourself require. Beneath the noise, beneath the wandering mind, Love continues waiting. Not demanding perfection, only your willingness to return once more.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *I return again to You*
>
> Exhale: *Beneath the noise within me*
