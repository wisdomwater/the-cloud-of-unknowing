## 75 - Trusting the God Who Calls

> *Where God truly leads, God also sustains.*

One of the great invitations of the spiritual life is learning to trust before every answer is known. The mind longs for certainty, wanting guarantees about the future before it is willing to surrender. Yet love rarely unfolds that way. When God calls you deeper, grace accompanies the call, providing what is needed for each step of the journey.

This does not mean life becomes free from difficulty. Challenges, questions, and seasons of uncertainty remain part of the human experience. Yet beneath them runs a deeper assurance that you are not carrying life alone. Divine presence does not always remove the burden, but it faithfully strengthens the heart that bears it.

Trust grows alongside discernment. Not every impulse leads toward life, and not every desire reflects the voice of God. A genuine invitation often carries a quiet peace, even when it asks for courage or sacrifice. As you learn to listen more deeply, you become better able to recognize the difference between restless ambition and authentic guidance.

Most of the spiritual journey is lived through ordinary faithfulness. You return to prayer, offer your love, and remain open to grace day by day. Sometimes moments of profound clarity arrive as unexpected gifts. More often, God works quietly beneath the surface, shaping your heart through steady devotion and simple trust.

You do not need to compare your path with anyone else's. Each soul is invited to express love in its own way and according to its own calling. Your task is simply to be faithful where you are, remain open to where God may lead, and trust the pace of grace. The mystery will always remain, but you need not fear it, for the goal is not to possess God, but to be carried ever more deeply into love.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Lead me in trust*
>
> Exhale: *I rest in Your care*
