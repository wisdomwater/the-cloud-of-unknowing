## 13 - Love Makes the Heart Spacious

> *The deeper the soul rests in God, the less tightly it clings to itself alone.*

As contemplative prayer deepens, the heart begins to widen. What once felt like a journey centered mostly on your own healing and longing slowly expands to include others. You begin seeing beneath appearances and recognizing that every person carries hidden burdens, fears, and hopes. Compassion grows naturally when you remember how much grace you yourself have needed.

The more deeply you rest in Love, the harder it becomes to divide the world into enemies and allies, insiders and outsiders. This does not remove the need for wisdom, boundaries, or discernment. But beneath life's differences and conflicts, you begin sensing a shared humanity. The heart quietly learns to desire the good of all, even those who misunderstand or wound you.

This spaciousness cannot be forced. It grows slowly as you return again and again to the presence of God. In that stillness, you discover that every person stands on equal ground—fragile, dependent, and held within a mystery greater than themselves. Mercy becomes easier to offer when you remember that you live by mercy too.

Yet the path is not without effort. The mind fills with plans, memories, worries, and distractions, each demanding your attention. The work of contemplation is not to fight these thoughts but to gently release them. Each time you notice yourself wandering, you simply return to Love with patience and kindness.

Over time, what once felt difficult begins to soften. The heart settles more easily, and moments of stillness arise with less striving. You discover that the deepest movement of prayer was never something you created through your own effort. God was already present beneath the noise, patiently drawing you inward through love from the very beginning.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Quiet my restless heart*
>
> Exhale: *Teach me deeper love*
