## 54 - When Words Fall Silent

> *The closer we come to God, the fewer words we need.*

The spiritual journey often begins with learning. Sacred teachings, scripture, prayer, and reflection help awaken the heart and turn it toward God. Words become faithful companions along the path, giving shape to what we sense and long for. Every true word about God is a gift that helps guide us toward deeper communion.

Yet there comes a point where words can carry you no further. The closer you draw to divine mystery, the more you realize that God is greater than every description. The mind reaches the edge of what it can explain, while the heart senses a depth that cannot be contained in language. What once seemed sufficient begins to feel wonderfully incomplete.

This is why contemplation gradually becomes a practice of listening rather than speaking. You may call God good, wise, loving, beautiful, and holy, and all of these are true. Yet God is always more than your understanding of goodness, wisdom, love, beauty, or holiness. Every sacred name points toward the Divine, but none can fully contain the One they describe.

As love deepens, you learn to hold even your most cherished ideas with open hands. You release false images, limiting assumptions, and the need to grasp God through certainty. With each surrender, the horizon widens. What first felt like losing understanding slowly becomes an entrance into wonder.

Eventually, the soul discovers a place beyond explanation. There is no argument to win, no conclusion to reach, and no mystery to solve. There is only Presence—quiet, radiant, and alive. The silence beyond words is not empty at all; it is filled with God.
