## 10 - The Quiet Work Hidden From Others

> *Not every sacred thing can be explained to those who have not yet felt it.*

There are seasons when the soul becomes less interested in striving and more drawn toward stillness. You find yourself longing for silence, prayer, and a deeper awareness of God's presence. From the outside, this may not look impressive. In fact, some people may not understand it at all.

Even those who care about you may question why you spend time in quiet prayer instead of visible activity. Yet the contemplative path has always been hidden in this way. Much of its work takes place beneath the surface, where transformation unfolds silently and cannot easily be measured or explained.

The story of Martha and Mary gently reveals this tension. Service, responsibility, and action are important expressions of love. Yet there are also moments when the soul is invited simply to sit, listen, and receive. What appears unproductive from the outside may be nurturing something deep and lasting within.

When others misunderstand your longing for stillness, resist the urge to become defensive. Not everyone has experienced the hunger that draws the soul inward. Meet misunderstanding with gentleness, and trust that not every sacred movement needs to be explained. Some truths are better lived than argued.

There may even be times when you question the value of this hidden work yourself. In those moments, remember that seeds grow in darkness and roots deepen out of sight. Each quiet return to prayer, each simple act of availability, allows love to work within you. Slowly and quietly, you are being changed in ways that only time will reveal.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Teach me quiet trust*
>
> Exhale: *I rest unseen in Love*
